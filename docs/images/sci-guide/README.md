# sci user guide screenshots

Six actual screenshots of the running sci/APW development reader, captured on
October 29, 2026 at the browser's normal 1280 by 720 viewport. They support the
[concise introduction](../../sci-overview.md) and
[detailed user guide](../../sci-user-guide.md).

| Image | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `01-paper-workspace.jpg` | Three papers, the original PDF and saved evidence highlights |
| `02-find-evidence.jpg` | An imported-evidence result from a different project paper, including its review label and passage link |
| `03-compare-findings.jpg` | An existing comparison entry with origin, conditions and unreviewed status |
| `04-project-changes.jpg` | A completed change check showing matching records and remaining review attention |
| `05-source-linked-brief.jpg` | Actual evidence counts, download control and supporting passage links |
| `06-saved-discovery.jpg` | A saved Crossref DOI result with its retrieval date and repeat-search control |

The reader uses APW revision `3ea2a5290f25dbc86a7ea178d0c0ed92849b2ba0`.
The project is the existing
[three-paper pilot](../../../evaluations/living-project-pilot-2026-10-28/README.md).
Its saved literature interpretations remain agent-origin and unreviewed.
The screenshots are unedited browser captures. Reading position, tabs, search
and saved selections were navigated for the illustrations; no scientific review
was entered, no new paper was selected and no analysis result was invented.

## Article attribution

The center panel shows page 1 of Zimmerman, Espeland and Langefeld (2021),
*A practical solution to pseudoreplication bias in single-cell studies*,
[DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-21038-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-21038-1).
Its text is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The application displays retained highlights over the original PDF; the source
article text was not altered.

The evidence-search result quotes Squair et al. (2021),
*Confronting false discoveries in single-cell differential expression*,
[DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-25960-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-25960-2),
also licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The paper list additionally identifies Crowell et al. (2020),
*muscat detects subpopulation-specific state transitions from multi-sample
multi-condition single-cell transcriptomics data*,
[DOI 10.1038/s41467-020-19894-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-19894-4).

The pilot's retained source provenance records the article licenses. Bibliographic
information in saved discovery comes from the displayed Crossref record, retrieved
